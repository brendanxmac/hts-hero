# E-commerce app stores and seller marketplaces as distribution channels for HTS Hero

Research date: 2026-10-26. About 20 searches and fetches. The Shopify App Store search pages render client-side and came back empty when fetched ("We are having trouble loading your results"), so the Shopify app list below comes from search-engine results restricted to apps.shopify.com, plus one listing fetched directly (DutyCalc). Ratings and review counts are as cached by search engines and may be a few weeks old.

## Which stores have shown demand for tariff tools in 2025-2026, and what already exists there?

### Takeaway
Shopify is the only store with a visible cluster of tariff-specific apps launched after the 2025 tariff changes, and almost all of them are thin. The ones with real traction are checkout surcharge apps that pass a "tariff fee" on to shoppers (about 90 to 200 reviews each). The importer-side tools, the category HTS Hero belongs in (catalog tariff exposure, HTS/COO hygiene, landed cost), have 0 to 2 reviews. On Amazon, demand is served by seller-suite vendors (Helium 10's Tariff Analyzer) rather than by Appstore listings. BigCommerce's duty apps are cross-border checkout (DDP) tools, not US import tools.

### Cited Findings
**Shopify: tariff surcharge and pass-through apps (demand proven, but this is a different job)**
- Magical Fees & Tariffs / Magical Surcharge + Extra Fees: 4.8 from 88 to 94 reviews on one listing name, 4.9 from 136 reviews on another. One reviewer praises it as easy to maintain "through all the constant US tariff changes". — [Listing](https://apps.shopify.com/magical-mandatory-fees); [Reviews](https://apps.shopify.com/reviews/2045514)
- Upcharge Surcharge Fee Tariff (UC Lab LLC): 4.7 from 197 reviews, about 8% one-star; some reviews say fees stopped working. — [Listing](https://apps.shopify.com/product-fees); [Reviews](https://apps.shopify.com/product-fees/reviews)
- Tariff Support by Intelligems: breaks tariff charges out for shoppers; 3.5 from 2 reviews. — [Listing](https://apps.shopify.com/tariff-support-by-intelligems); [Reviews](https://apps.shopify.com/tariff-support-by-intelligems/reviews)
- Tariffed (Company27 LLC): shows estimated tariff costs on product pages; $3.47/month; launched June 19, 2025; 0 reviews. — [Listing](https://apps.shopify.com/tariffed); [Ecommerce Fastlane coverage](https://ecommercefastlane.com/tariffed-shopify-app/)

**Shopify: importer-side tariff exposure, HTS and landed-cost tools (HTS Hero's category)**
- DutyCalc (developer "US Tariff Rates"): launched May 19, 2026; 0 reviews. It scans the catalog for missing HS/HTS and country-of-origin data, then shows base duty, Section 301, Section 232, reciprocal tariffs and AD/CVD risk per SKU, and models landed duty and margin impact. Reviewed HTS/origin can be written back to products. Free (10 lookups/month), Pro $49/month (1,000), Team $199/month (10,000). Scopes: view store owner data; view and edit products. The listing does not mention alerts or refunds. — [Listing (fetched)](https://apps.shopify.com/dutycalc)
- Tariff HS Code Compliance: keeps HS codes current as the catalog changes; 5.0 from 1 review. — [Listing](https://apps.shopify.com/tariff-code-compliance)
- Flash AI HS Classification (FlavorCloud): AI HS codes; launched October 29, 2025; no rating shown. — [Listing](https://apps.shopify.com/flash-ai-hs-classification)
- Saifion: true import cost per unit (goods + freight + duty), written to variant metafields. — [Listing](https://apps.shopify.com/saifion-logistics)
- True COGS: landed unit cost including freight and duty, from $29/month; a reviewer says the tariff page is the part they use most. — [Listing](https://apps.shopify.com/truecogs)
- ProfitDeck: prices purchase orders with duty at each line's HS rate, from $19/month. — [Listing](https://apps.shopify.com/profitdeck)
- DropShip Intelligence: estimates duty, VAT and shipping from China to 15 countries before ordering; free tier. — [Listing](https://apps.shopify.com/dropship-intelligence)

**Shopify: cross-border checkout duty apps (outbound, not US import)**
- Swap Global (guaranteed duties and taxes at checkout, DDP), Anbeond Duty and Tax ($149/month plus per-order fees), Tax & Duty King (landed cost in cart, from $19.99/month for 750 calculations), Calcurates. — [Swap](https://apps.shopify.com/swap-global); [Anbeond](https://apps.shopify.com/anbeond); [Tax & Duty King](https://apps.shopify.com/tax-duty-king); [Calcurates](https://apps.shopify.com/calcurates)

**Amazon**
- Helium 10 launched a Tariff Analyzer in 2025, inside the Profits tab: pick a SKU and origin country to see tariff cost against COGS, compare margins before and after, and model sourcing alternatives such as Vietnam or Mexico. It comes with a Tariff Strategy Center. — [Helium 10 blog](https://www.helium10.com/blog/new-tariff-analyzer-tool/); [RevenueGeeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/helium-10-tariff/)
- My Amazon Guy (an agency) offers a U.S. Tariff-Price Calculator: landed cost per unit with HTS base duty, Section 301 and Amazon fees. Not confirmed as an Appstore listing. — [My Amazon Guy](https://myamazonguy.com/?p=108042)
- Amazon's own answer for sellers is Amazon Customs & Trade (licensed brokers; HTS classification and PGA expertise) plus the Service Provider Network. — [Amazon Customs & Trade](https://spcentral.amazon.com/nlp/customs-and-trade); [About Amazon](https://aboutamazon.com/news/retail/how-amazon-is-supporting-sellers-through-global-trade-changes)
- AMZScout's PRO AI extension includes a profit calculator for modeling margins under new tariffs. — [AMZScout](https://amzscout.net/blog/amazon-tariffs-guide/)
- Jungle Scout publishes a "Tariff Index 2025" report. No Jungle Scout tariff tool was found. — [Jungle Scout](https://www.junglescout.com/resources/reports/tariff-index-2025/)

**BigCommerce**
- Dutify (AI HS classification; duty and tax for 110+ destinations; went live on the BigCommerce marketplace June 17, 2026; supports Section 232 and FTA rates), EdgeTariff (DDP checkout landed cost), OpenBorder Tax & Duties (custom pricing), Zonos Duty and Tax, and UPS Global Checkout. All are checkout and cross-border oriented. — [Dutify](https://www.bigcommerce.com/apps/dutify-duty-tax/); [Dutify changelog](https://docs.dutify.com/changelog/bigcommerce-dutify-app-v1); [EdgeTariff](https://bigcommerce.com/apps/edgetariff); [OpenBorder](https://www.bigcommerce.com/apps/openborder-tax/); [Zonos](https://zonos.com/docs/global-ecommerce/integrating-landed-cost/bigcommerce); [UPS](https://www.bigcommerce.com/apps/ups-global-checkout/)

**Enterprise and adjacent launches**
- Project44 Tariff Analytics (September 25, 2025) maps product catalogs to live US tariff rates. Beroe launched tariff monitoring on August 19, 2025. Both are enterprise products, not app-store products. — [Cottrill Research](https://cottrillresearch.com/new-tariff-solution-launch-announcements/)

### Inferences
- Shopify merchants have clearly been dealing with tariffs since 2025: the surcharge apps gained reviews and now name "tariff" in their titles. But that demand has mostly gone to "pass the cost to the customer" tools. Importer-side exposure and audit tools are a new, unproven category, with no app above 2 reviews found.
- Nothing found on any store combines tariff-change alerts with a refund or overpayment audit (IEEPA, Section 301 exclusions, FTA/GSP, first sale). DutyCalc is the closest competitor: same positioning, launched May 2026, 0 reviews. That makes the slot open but also unvalidated.
- On Amazon, sellers get tariff modeling from the suites they already pay for (Helium 10). A standalone app would have to beat a bundled feature.

### Gaps
- Install counts: Shopify does not publish installs, and no third-party tracker data (Store Leads, ShopifyAppStoreStats, etc.) was retrieved.
- A live Shopify App Store search for "tariff", "duty", "HTS", "landed cost" and "customs" could not be scraped. The full result counts per query are unknown, so the list above may be incomplete.
- No Amazon Selling Partner Appstore listing for a tariff or duty app was found; the Appstore is browsed from inside Seller Central and could not be searched.
- WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace, Etsy, eBay, Walmart, TikTok Shop, Faire and Adobe Commerce stores were not searched for tariff apps app-by-app (tool budget ran out). No tariff-specific apps surfaced for them in general searches.
- Growth over time in review counts for the tariff apps (for example, before and after April 2025) was not measured.

## Audience size: merchants on each platform and how many import into the US

### Takeaway
Shopify (44% of merchants are in the US, roughly 2 to 3 million US stores by third-party estimates) and Amazon (about 38% of registered sellers are US-based, and Chinese sellers make up 35 to 50% or more of 3P revenue or top sellers) are by far the biggest pools. No platform publishes the share of merchants who import into the US themselves.

### Cited Findings
- Shopify FY2025 10-K: "millions" of merchants in 175+ countries, 44% in the United States, as of December 31, 2025. No absolute count is given. — [Shopify 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1594805/000159480526000007/shop-20251231.htm)
- Third-party estimates put active Shopify stores at about 4.82 million to 6.52 million (November 2025), with about 2.67 million in the US. Definitions vary. — [Omnisend](https://www.omnisend.com/blog/shopify-statistics/); [Ecommerce Fastlane](https://ecommercefastlane.com/shopify-statistics-2026-latest-usage-sales-and-trends)
- Amazon: the US is about 37.82% of registered sellers (SmartScout). Third-party sellers account for about 69% of Amazon's estimated 2025 global GMV (SellerSprite). — [SmartScout](https://smartscout.com/blog/amazon-seller-statistics); [SellerSprite](https://www.sellersprite.com/en/blog/amazon-2025-annual-data-report)
- Chinese sellers: 48.9% of 3P revenue and 35.1% of total revenue in one dataset; Marketplace Pulse says more than 50% of top sellers on Amazon.com are Chinese, with US sellers at about 45% (and some foreign sellers incorporate in the US). — [Marketplace Pulse](https://www.marketplacepulse.com/articles/amazons-significant-reliance-on-chinese-sellers); [Marketplace Pulse 2](https://marketplacepulse.com/articles/american-sellers-lost-amazon-to-china); [Nova Data](https://novadata.io/resources/amazon-seller-statistics)
- Wix had 25M users at its App Market launch, an old figure (around 2014). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=675002)
- Amazon's Appstore lists 2,500+ apps across 20+ countries (per Amazon regional pages). — [Amazon CA](https://sell.amazon.ca/grow/selling-partner-appstore); [SP-API docs](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/lang-US/docs/what-is-the-selling-partner-appstore)

### Inferences
- For a US importer-of-record ICP, Shopify is the better fit: US-based brands that buy from overseas suppliers and import themselves. A large share of Amazon's top sellers are China-based and often ship DDP or through a forwarder acting as IOR, so they are less likely to file their own CBP entries or claim refunds.
- A rough Shopify TAM (inference, not sourced): if even 5 to 10% of about 2.7 million US stores import physical goods themselves, that is 100k to 270k candidate merchants.

### Gaps
- No source quantifies the share of Shopify or Amazon sellers who are US importers of record.
- Current merchant counts for WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Wix, Squarespace, Etsy, eBay, Walmart, TikTok Shop, Faire and Adobe Commerce were not retrieved within budget. A WooCommerce store-count source exists but was not opened ([Red Stag](https://redstagfulfillment.com/how-many-woocommerce-stores-are-live/)).

## Which stores are least crowded relative to audience size?

### Takeaway
For HTS Hero's specific job (importer-side exposure, alerts and refund audit), Shopify is both the biggest audience and nearly empty: DutyCalc, Tariff HS Code Compliance and a few COGS/landed-cost apps, all with 0 to 2 reviews on the tariff-specific ones. BigCommerce's duty category is full of outbound checkout tools, with no importer-exposure tool found. Amazon's Appstore has no tariff listing found, but bundled suites already compete there.

### Cited Findings
- See the app lists and review counts in the first section: DutyCalc 0 reviews ([link](https://apps.shopify.com/dutycalc)); Tariff HS Code Compliance 1 review ([link](https://apps.shopify.com/tariff-code-compliance)); Tariffed 0 reviews ([link](https://apps.shopify.com/tariffed)). Against these, the surcharge apps have 94 to 197 reviews.
- BigCommerce duty apps are all checkout/DDP (Dutify, EdgeTariff, OpenBorder, Zonos, UPS). — [Dutify](https://www.bigcommerce.com/apps/dutify-duty-tax/); [EdgeTariff](https://bigcommerce.com/apps/edgetariff)

### Inferences
- Ranking for HTS Hero (inference): 1) Shopify: large US audience, rich product data (HTS, COO and cost on the inventory item), few real competitors. 2) Amazon Appstore/SPN: large audience, but the API lacks the data and bundled suites compete. 3) BigCommerce and WooCommerce: smaller audiences, but a nearly empty import-side category. 4) Wix, Squarespace, Etsy, eBay, TikTok Shop, Faire: small or poor-fit audiences (makers, resellers, or China-based sellers) or weak developer channels.
- "Uncrowded" partly reflects unproven demand. The surcharge apps' traction suggests merchants want something that acts at checkout, while exposure and refund tools need education.

### Gaps
- No quantitative competitor counts per store for WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace, Adobe Commerce, eBay, Walmart, TikTok Shop or Faire.

## What data does each platform's API expose (cost, HS code, country of origin)?

### Takeaway
Shopify is the strongest: the InventoryItem has `harmonizedSystemCode`, `countryCodeOfOrigin`, `countryHarmonizedSystemCodes` and `unitCost`, both readable and writable. That enables automatic import of SKU, HTS, COO and cost, landed cost per product, and write-back. The other platforms were not verified in detail.

### Cited Findings
- Shopify Admin GraphQL `inventoryItems` returns `countryCodeOfOrigin`, `harmonizedSystemCode`, `unitCost` (an amount plus currency, possibly null) and `countryHarmonizedSystemCodes`. These fields are stable from 2022-10 through 2026-07. For writes, `InventoryItemInput` uses `cost` (a Decimal), not `unitCost`; `countryCodeOfOrigin` is ISO alpha-2 and `harmonizedSystemCode` is a string. — [inventoryItems (2026-07)](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-07/queries/inventoryItems); [InventoryItemInput](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/input-objects/InventoryItemInput)
- DutyCalc's listing shows the scopes such an app asks for: view store owner data, plus view and edit products and collections. — [DutyCalc](https://apps.shopify.com/dutycalc)
- Dutify connects to BigCommerce through the Shipping Provider API, which is a checkout hook, not catalog sync. — [Dutify docs](https://docs.dutify.com/docs/bigcommerce)
- Walmart: OAuth 2.0 with API scopes chosen at app registration. — [Walmart dev](https://developer.walmart.com/us-marketplace/docs/app-registration-and-approval-process-for-publishing-to-app-store)
- Etsy API v3: REST/JSON with OAuth 2.0; app registration through the Etsy Developer Portal. Origin and HS fields were not confirmed. — [Frihet guide](https://docs.frihet.io/en/integraciones/etsy)

### Inferences
- With Shopify, HTS Hero could, on install, read every variant's HTS, COO and cost, flag missing ones (which is DutyCalc's pitch), compute duty and landed cost per SKU, and show tariff-change alerts in the admin (via an embedded app page and email). The refund audit still needs CBP entry data (ACE reports or 7501s), which no store API provides. So the audit stays an upload flow on the HTS Hero site.
- With Amazon, SP-API gives listings, fees and orders, but sellers' COGS and HTS are not standard SP-API fields (from background knowledge, not verified this session), so users would enter them manually or upload a file.

### Gaps
- Not verified: BigCommerce product fields (BigCommerce is believed to have customs-information endpoints with HS codes and country of origin, but these were not checked), WooCommerce (no native HS/COO fields; typically plugin meta), Amazon SP-API HTS/COO fields, eBay Inventory API, TikTok Shop, Walmart item spec fields, Faire, Adobe Commerce.

## How hard and expensive is listing for a solo founder?

### Takeaway
Shopify is cheap and fast: a one-time $19 fee, 0% revenue share on the first $1M lifetime and 15% after, with review in about 1 to 2 weeks (sometimes 5 or more). Amazon dropped its planned $1,400/year SP-API fee in May 2026 (per secondary sources), but Appstore vetting is heavier. Walmart takes 3 to 5 weeks for solution-provider approval. BigCommerce charges a review and listing fee. WooCommerce takes a cut of marketplace sales.

### Cited Findings
- Shopify: one-time $19 registration to join the app revenue-share program. — [Shopify Partners glossary](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/partner-program/glossary)
- Shopify revenue share: since June 16, 2025, 0% on the first US$1M of lifetime revenue per developer (revenue before January 1, 2025 does not count), then 15%. This replaced the annual $1M reset from 2021. — [Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/32186933/shopify-developers-lose-annual-revenue-share-break-as-program-moves-to-lifetime-model); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2171395)
- Shopify review time: third-party guides say 4 to 14 business days; forum reports describe backlogs of 5 or more weeks. — [Shopify dev community](https://community.shopify.dev/t/longer-app-store-review-times-what-you-need-to-know/31728?page=4); [Growave](https://www.growave.io/it/blog/how-long-does-shopify-app-review-take)
- Amazon SP-API: a $1,400 annual fee (from January 31, 2026) plus GET-call usage tiers (from April 30, 2026) were announced for third-party developers. Secondary sources say Amazon emailed developers on May 12, 2026 that it would not go ahead "at this time". The official page still showed the original schedule when the search ran. — [Amazon announcement](https://developer.amazonservices.com/spp-announcement); [PPC Land (fees dropped)](https://ppc.land/amazon-drops-sp-api-fees-after-developer-pushback); [Nova Data](https://novadata.io/resources/news/amazon-cancels-sp-api-fees-may-2026)
- Amazon Appstore: Amazon vets apps at onboarding and on an ongoing basis; partners set their own prices. — [SP-API docs](https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/lang-US/docs/what-is-the-selling-partner-appstore)
- Amazon SPN has 19 service categories; Germany's page lists "Trade compliance". No US tariff-specific category was found. — [SPN categories](https://developer.amazonservices.com/service-provider-network/understanding-spn-service-categories-where-does-your-business-fit); [SPN DE](https://sell.amazon.de/en/spn)
- Walmart: solution-provider application, approval in 3 to 5 weeks, sandbox and OAuth 2.0, then app registration with general/marketing and technical sections and verification. — [Walmart get started](https://developer.walmart.com/us-marketplace/docs/get-started-as-a-solution-provider); [Walmart publish](https://developer.walmart.com/us-marketplace/docs/app-registration-and-approval-process-for-publishing-to-app-store)
- BigCommerce: "Submit app for Review" requires paying a review and listing fee (amount not shown in snippet); PCI review applies if the app handles payments. A secondary article says BigCommerce takes no revenue cut. — [BigCommerce publishing](https://docs.bigcommerce.com/developer/docs/integrations/apps/guide/publishing-apps); [Approval requirements](https://docs.bigcommerce.com/developer/docs/integrations/apps/guide/approval-requirements); [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/bigcommerce-aims-to-improve-its-app-ecosystem/)
- WooCommerce marketplace: the revenue share for developers was raised from 50% to 60% (developer keeps 60%; date unclear, likely years old), and the developer handles all support. — [Post Status](https://poststatus.com/?p=37308)
- Wix: originally 70/30 for the developer; at DevStudio 2024 Wix offered 100% revenue share in a developer's first year. — [Computerworld](https://www.computerworld.com/article/1528558/wix-com-launches-apps-market.html); [Small Biz Trends](https://smallbiztrends.com/wix-unveils-new-features-at-devstudio-conference-to-empower-app-developers/)
- Squarespace: a developer platform and extensions marketplace exist; no submission criteria or revenue split were found. — [Squarespace developers](https://developers.squarespace.com/)
- TikTok Shop: Partner Center offers public apps (for the App Store) and custom apps; setup asks for name, category, target market and redirect URL (third-party guide). — [unified.to](https://unified.to/blog/how_to_setup_a_tiktok_shop_application.md); [TikTok dev blog](https://developers.tiktok.com/blog/tiktok-shop-developer-updates)
- Adobe Commerce Marketplace has a seller guide on business value; terms were not retrieved. — [Adobe](https://developer.adobe.com/commerce/marketplace/guides/sellers/business-value)

### Inferences
- For a solo founder, Shopify has the lowest friction for the highest fit. A first listing could be a narrow embedded app: catalog HTS/COO health plus duty and tariff exposure per SKU, with alerts. It would link out to the HTS Hero Tracker for the audit, and to sign-up for accounts.
- Shopify's review rules generally require Shopify Billing for in-app charges (background knowledge, not verified this session). That may conflict with HTS Hero's Stripe-based Tracker pricing and needs checking before building.
- Amazon is lower priority until the fee situation is settled and a data gap is solved; the SPN (a service directory) might be an easier presence than a full Appstore app.

### Gaps
- BigCommerce's exact listing fee, current WooCommerce terms (2025-2026), Squarespace and Faire partner terms, eBay and Etsy app directories, Adobe Commerce revenue share, and Shopify Collective (which is not an app store; no data gathered) were not confirmed.
- Shopify Billing API requirements and "Built for Shopify" criteria for 2026 were not checked.
