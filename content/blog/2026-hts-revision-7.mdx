Revision 7 is short: three changes, all in the Section 232 metals notes, and all reaching back to April 6, 2026. That's the day the current `9903.82` headings started. The practical effect lands on one product group: insulated copper wire and cable.

## What changed

### Copper articles can use the 10% U.S.-smelted rate

U.S. note 16(e) used to say that heading `9903.82.06` applies to articles in subdivisions (c)(ii), (iv), (vi) and (vii). Revision 7 deleted that sentence.

With it gone, `9903.82.06` reaches everything its own heading text names: subdivisions (c)(ii), (iv), (vi) to (viii), and (e). Subdivision (c)(viii) is the copper list:

- `8544.42.10`, `8544.42.20` and `8544.42.90`
- `8544.49.10`

So copper cable made with at least 95% U.S.-smelted copper now pays the regular duty plus **10%** under `9903.82.06`. Without that, it stays under `9903.82.09` at the regular duty plus **25%**.

### Heading 9903.82.01 added for articles with no metal

New heading `9903.82.01` covers "articles provided for in subdivision (c) of U.S. note 16 ... that do not contain any aluminum, steel, or copper". It says "No change" in every rate column, so qualifying goods pay no Section 232 metals duty. Its scope is all of note 16(c), which includes the copper articles in (c)(viii).

### Note 16(a) lists the new exemption

Note 16(a) now opens "Except as provided in headings `9903.82.01`, `9903.85.67`, and `9903.85.68`". In other words, an article that qualifies for `9903.82.01` doesn't also enter under one of `9903.82.02` to `9903.82.19`. Only one of those headings applies to an article.

<Callout tone="primary" title="Retroactive to April 6">
Because every change is effective April 6, 2026, it also covers entries made under Revisions 5 and 6. Copper cable entered from April 6 to April 28 at 25% may have qualified for 10%.
</Callout>

## What it means for importers

**On $10,000 of insulated copper cable** under `8544.42.90` (2.6% regular duty) from Vietnam:

| Copper content | Heading | Total duty |
|---|---|---|
| Not shown to be U.S.-smelted | `9903.82.09`: 2.6% + 25% | $2,760 |
| At least 95% U.S.-smelted and cast | `9903.82.06`: 2.6% + 10% | $1,260 |

That's $1,500 less per $10,000 for qualifying cable. A no-metal article under `9903.82.01` pays no Section 232 duty at all, although other duties, such as Section 122, can still apply.

If you import copper wire or cable, check your entries from April 6 onward. Make sure you have mill or smelter records that support the 95% U.S.-smelted claim before you rely on it.
