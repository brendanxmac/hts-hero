2026 HTS Revision 20 is a pharmaceuticals revision, in force from September 28, 2026, with changes that apply from September 29. It leaves every rate alone. It changes **which products** the [Section 232 pharmaceutical tariff](/blog/2026-hts-revision-14) covers and **which ones are exempt**, which for a drug importer can mean the difference between 0% and 100%. As of October 6, 2026, it's the current revision of the HTS.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Revision** | 2026 HTS Revision 20 |
| **In force from** | September 28, 2026 |
| **Tariff changes effective** | September 29, 2026 |
| **Programs affected** | Section 232 pharmaceuticals |
| **Previous revision** | [Revision 19](/blog/2026-hts-revision-19) |


## What changed

### 149 lines on the pharmaceutical tariff list

The Section 232 pharmaceutical tariff applies to products listed in U.S. note 40(c) to Chapter 99. Revision 20 expanded that list from **131 to 149** statistical lines:

- Several existing lines were split into finer statistical numbers, including `2922.19.09`, `2934.99.47`, `2937.22.00`, `2941.90.30`, `2941.90.50` and `3004.31.00`.
- In Chapter 30, four lines were removed (including `3004.20.0083` and `3004.90.9225`) and 14 were added under `3004.20`, `3004.32`, `3004.39` and `3004.90.92`.

A medicine classified under one of the newly added lines that paid no Section 232 duty on September 28 can owe up to 100% from September 29, unless an exemption or a country deal applies.

### New exemption 9903.04.70: clinical trials, R&D and non-commercial use

New heading `9903.04.70` covers pharmaceutical articles and ingredients that are **solely for use in clinical trials, research and development, or other non-commercial applications**, at 0%.

It joins the other "not a patented pharmaceutical" exemptions, for generics (`9903.04.67`), U.S. active ingredients (`9903.04.68`) and articles that aren't pharmaceuticals (`9903.04.69`). The headings from `9903.04.60` to `9903.04.70` are mutually exclusive, so a product enters under exactly one.

<Callout tone="warning" title="The country rate can still apply">
Goods under `9903.04.70` don't count as Section 232 articles for the country programs, so the Section 301 forced-labor duty for the country of origin applies unless another exemption covers the product. Nearly all finished medicines under heading 3004 (every subheading except `3004.43.00`) are on note 52(b)'s list and pay no country rate. A listed active ingredient such as [`2918.99.30.00`](/hts/2918.99.30.00) from India, used solely for research, pays India's 10% unless it's claimed under `9903.05.89` for pharmaceutical applications.
</Callout>

### Lapsed: the "identified companies" exemption

`9903.04.61`, which exempted products of companies identified by the Secretary of Commerce, only covered goods entered before September 29, 2026. That end date was written into the heading from the start in [Revision 14](/blog/2026-hts-revision-14), and it fell on the same day Revision 20's changes began. Those products now fall under whichever other pharmaceutical heading fits.

### Clarified definitions

The notes also clarified three definitions without changing any heading:

- Excipients and inactive ingredients aren't pharmaceutical articles.
- Unpatented animal health products count as generics.
- `9903.04.69` also covers pharmaceutical articles that are neither patented nor generic.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Also from September 29: Canadian import bans">
Proclamations 11061, 11062 and 11063 ban imports of certain Canadian products listed in their annexes from 12:01 a.m. eastern time on September 29, 2026: many beers, wines and spirits (some only when packaged for consumers), whey, molasses and non-alcoholic beer, and motorcycles over 800 cc. CBP rejects any entry that includes them ([CSMS #70050970](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP/bulletins/42ce49a)). Goods imported before September 29 but not yet entered for consumption can still be entered, and they pay the 50% Section 338 duty. These bans came by proclamation and CBP guidance, outside this revision's change record.
</Callout>

## What it means for importers

**On $10,000 of a patented medicine** under [`3004.90.92.60`](/hts/3004.90.92.60) (base rate free), entered from September 29, 2026:

| Origin and use | Heading | Duty |
|---|---|---|
| Germany, commercial | `9903.04.62`: 15% including base | $1,500 |
| India, commercial | `9903.04.60`: up to 100% including base | $10,000 |
| India, solely for clinical trials | `9903.04.70` (0%), and 3004.90.92 is exempt from the country rate under note 52(b) | $0 |

If you import pharmaceuticals or their ingredients, check two things against Revision 20: whether any of your HTS lines were added to (or dropped from) the note 40(c) list, and whether any shipments qualify for the new R&D exemption. The [duty calculator](/duty-calculator) applies the Revision 20 list by entry date and asks whether the R&D exemption fits.
