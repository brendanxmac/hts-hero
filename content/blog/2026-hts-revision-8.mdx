2026 HTS Revision 8 changed no tariff rates. Its one Chapter 99 edit makes the 25% Section 232 duty on medium- and heavy-duty truck and bus parts subject to an import adjustment offset that Commerce can set for each manufacturer. No headings were added, and the rest of the revision touches general notes and Chapter 98.

## What changed in Revision 8

### Truck and bus parts duty is now "subject to" an offset

U.S. note 38(i) lists the medium- and heavy-duty vehicle parts that pay the duty in heading `9903.74.08`. Before Revision 8, it simply said that heading's rate applies to those parts. It now begins:

> Subject to a manufacturer's import adjustment offset amount that may be determined by the Secretary of Commerce under Proclamation 10984 of October 17, 2025 (90 FR 48451) ...

The change is effective November 1, 2025. Two things stayed the same:

- **The parts list.** It has the same 183 codes before and after.
- **The rate.** `9903.74.08` is still the regular duty plus 25%.

Auto parts under note 33(g) already carry the same offset wording, from Proclamation 10925 of April 29, 2025 (90 FR 18899).

<Callout tone="primary" title="The offset isn't a published rate">
Commerce sets an offset for a specific manufacturer. You can't read it off the HTS, and it doesn't depend on the HTS code or country. If your manufacturer has no approved offset, the full 25% applies.
</Callout>

### Other items outside Chapter 99

The change record also lists edits from Proclamation 11030, which implements provisions of the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2026:

| Item | Effective |
|---|---|
| General note 16(a) | January 1, 2026 |
| General note 16(c) | October 1, 2025 |
| U.S. note 2(b), subchapter XIX, Chapter 98 | October 1, 2025 |
| U.S. notes 6(f)(i) and 6(g)(i), subchapter XX, Chapter 98 | December 20, 2025 |
| Heading `9819.11.12` | October 1, 2025 |

It also fixed a Schedule C code for Burma in Statistical Annex A (546.1 back to 546.0). None of these change a Chapter 99 duty.

## What it means for truck parts importers

**On $10,000 of a medium- or heavy-duty vehicle part** under [`8708.99.68`](/hts/8708.99.68) (2.5% regular duty) from Germany, `9903.74.08` adds 25%, or **$2,500**, before any offset. A Commerce-approved offset for the manufacturer can bring that down. Without one, nothing changes from Revision 7.

If you import truck or bus parts, ask your suppliers whether they have an import adjustment offset from Commerce, and how it should be documented on your entries. For the full list of 2026 revisions, see the [2026 HTS revisions hub](/blog/2026-hts-revisions).
