Revision 16 adds a new safeguard. Under Proclamation 11051, imports of quartz surface products pay an additional Section 201 duty from August 15, 2026, with a quarterly tariff-rate quota deciding whether the rate is 25% or 50%. It's set to run four years, with a small step down each August.

## What changed

### What's covered

New U.S. note 41 to Chapter 99 defines quartz surface products: goods made mostly of silica (more than any other single material, by weight) with a resin binder, sold as slabs, countertops, tiles and similar. Quarried stone is excluded.

The scope covers three statistical lines:

- `6810.99.0020`
- `6810.99.0040`
- `7020.00.6000`

Not every product under these lines is a quartz surface product. That's especially true of `7020.00.6000`, the catch-all for other articles of glass. If your goods don't meet the note 41(a) definition, the safeguard doesn't apply. When quartz is imported together with sinks, vanities or cabinets, only the quartz surface product is covered.

### Two headings, one quota

| Heading | When it applies | Rate (first year) |
|---|---|---|
| `9903.45.30` | Entered within the quarterly quota | + 25% |
| `9903.45.31` | Entered after the quota is filled | + 50% |

The quota is set in square meters per quarter (3,251,606 square meters a quarter in the first year), and unused quantity carries forward. The same rate applies in every column, and note 41(b) makes the duty cumulative: it adds to the regular Chapter 68 or 70 duty and to other Chapter 99 duties, such as Section 301 for China or a country rate.

### Rates step down each year

| Entered | In quota (`9903.45.30`) | Over quota (`9903.45.31`) |
|---|---|---|
| Aug 15, 2026 to Aug 14, 2027 | 25% | 50% |
| Aug 15, 2027 to Aug 14, 2028 | 23% | 49% |
| Aug 15, 2028 to Aug 14, 2029 | 21% | 48% |
| Aug 15, 2029 to Aug 14, 2030 | 19% | 47% |

### Exempt countries

Note 41(c) exempts the products of:

- Canada and Mexico;
- 13 free-trade partners: Australia, Colombia, Costa Rica, the Dominican Republic, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Israel, Nicaragua, Panama, Peru, Singapore and South Korea;
- 100 listed developing countries, including Brazil;
- 17 Caribbean Basin countries and territories, including the Bahamas and Trinidad and Tobago.

<Callout tone="warning" title="China, India and Vietnam pay">
The big quartz suppliers outside North America aren't on the exempt list. For China, the safeguard stacks on top of Section 301 and the country rate.
</Callout>

Revision 16 also changed the unit of quantity for `7020.00.6000`, effective the same day.

## What it means for importers

**On $10,000 of quartz slab** under `6810.99.0020` from Vietnam:

| Entered | Headings | Additional duty |
|---|---|---|
| August 14, 2026 | None for Section 201 | No safeguard |
| August 20, 2026, in quota | `9903.45.30` 25% + Vietnam's 12.5% country rate | $3,750 |
| August 20, 2026, quota filled | `9903.45.31` 50% + Vietnam's 12.5% country rate | $6,250 |

The difference between in quota and over quota is $2,500 on every $10,000, and it depends on timing within each quarter. If you import quartz, track how quickly each quarter's quota fills, check your origin against the note 41(c) list, and confirm that your goods actually meet the quartz surface product definition before paying the duty on a glass or stone line.
