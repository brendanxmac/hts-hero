2026 HTS Revision 10 restructures the Section 232 steel, aluminum and copper tariffs from June 8, 2026, under Proclamation 11032. Many machinery and air conditioner parts move from 25% to 10% or 15% treatments, forklifts and earthmovers get their own list and rates, the U.S.-content threshold drops from 95% to 85%, and seven headings join the `9903.82` family. For many machinery parts, the duty goes down.

## What changed in Revision 10

### Products move between the note 16(c) lists

Which list a product sits on decides its Section 232 metals rate. Revision 10 reshuffled four lists and added a fifth:

| List | Change from June 8 |
|---|---|
| (c)(vi) aluminum derivatives | Four A/C parts codes move to (ix); `3701.30.00` added |
| (c)(vii) steel derivatives | 62 codes leave, for (x) and the new (xi); three `9403` codes added |
| (c)(ix) aluminum derivatives | Gains the four A/C parts codes from (vi) |
| (c)(x) steel derivatives | Gains 34 codes from (vii): A/C parts, tractors, agricultural machinery, some auto parts |
| (c)(xi) mobile industrial equipment (new) | 28 codes: forklifts and work trucks, earthmoving machines and their parts, certain tractors and special purpose vehicles |

Products that moved from (vii) to (x) leave the 25% treatment under `9903.82.09` for the lower 10% and 15% treatments of (ix) and (x).

### 85% U.S. content instead of 95%

Note 16(e) sets how much of an article's metal must be U.S.-melted or U.S.-smelted for the lower rates in `9903.82.06`, `9903.82.07`, `9903.82.08` and `9903.82.15`, and now in the new `9903.82.23` and `9903.82.24`. Revision 10 lowered that bar from **95% to 85%**.

### Seven new headings

| Heading | Covers | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| `9903.82.20` | (xi) equipment from Canada or Mexico under USMCA: the non-U.S. content and U.S. content above 40% | Regular duty + 25% |
| `9903.82.21` | Same equipment: U.S. content up to 40% of value | No extra duty |
| `9903.82.22` | (xi) equipment from Japan, Korea, Taiwan, Switzerland, the UK, the EU and five other partners | 15% |
| `9903.82.23` | Note 16(k) parts with 85% U.S. metal, regular duty under 10% | 10% total |
| `9903.82.24` | Same, regular duty 10% or more | No extra duty |
| `9903.82.25` | Note 16(k) parts without 85% U.S. metal, regular duty under 15% | 15% total |
| `9903.82.26` | Same, regular duty 15% or more | No extra duty |

New note 16(k) covers parts in Chapters 84, 85 and 87 from lists (vi) to (viii) that are used only to make agricultural equipment, fixed industrial equipment or mobile industrial equipment. The 16(k) headings don't apply to Column 2 countries.

`9903.82.09` now gives way to all seven new headings, and the cross-references in notes 33, 38 and 39 that ran from `9903.82.04` to `9903.82.19` now run to `9903.82.26`.

<Callout tone="primary" title="Section 122 and 9903.82.02">
Revision 10's note 2(aa)(v)(1) left `9903.82.02` out of the Section 122 exemption by mistake. A technical correction in Revision 11 restored it back to April 6, 2026, so steel and aluminum under `9903.82.02` didn't owe Section 122 in June.
</Callout>

## Section 232 metals duty examples from June 8, 2026

**On $10,000**, from June 8, 2026:

| Product | Treatment | Total duty |
|---|---|---|
| A/C part [`8415.10.60`](/hts/8415.10.60.00) from Vietnam (1% regular duty) | Was `9903.82.09`: 1% + 25%. Now `9903.82.10`: 15% total | $1,500 |
| Earthmover from Germany (free regular duty) | `9903.82.22`: 15% | $1,500 |
| Earthmover from Canada under USMCA, 20% U.S. content | 25% on the 80% that isn't U.S. content | $2,000 |
| Same, 60% U.S. content | 25% on the 40% non-U.S. content plus the 20% of U.S. content above the 40% cap | $1,500 |
| Machinery part [`8431.43.80`](/hts/8431.43.80) from Vietnam (free regular duty), for building industrial equipment | `9903.82.25`: 15% total | $1,500 |
| Same part with 85% U.S.-melted metal | `9903.82.23`: 10% total | $1,000 |

If you import machinery, A/C parts or equipment parts, check which note 16(c) list your codes are on from June 8. If you make agricultural or industrial equipment, document end use and U.S. metal content so you can claim the 16(k) rates.

## What changed after Revision 10

- **July 1, 2026:** [Revision 11](/blog/2026-hts-revision-11) corrected note 2(aa)(v)(1), restoring the Section 122 exemption for `9903.82.02` back to April 6, 2026.
- **July 24, 2026:** Section 122 expired and the Section 301 forced-labor duties began ([Revision 13](/blog/2026-hts-revision-13)). Goods under the Section 232 metals headings `9903.82.02` and `9903.82.04` to `9903.82.26` are exempt from those under `9903.05.90`.

For how Section 232 interacts with the other tariffs, see [US tariff stacking rules](/blog/us-tariff-stacking-rules), and for every 2026 revision, the [2026 HTS revisions hub](/blog/2026-hts-revisions).
