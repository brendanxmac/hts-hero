2026 HTS Revision 15 is a short one. Its change record has exactly one line, and it's good news for anyone bringing in patented medicines from the United Kingdom. The 10% Section 232 rate that applied to them under heading `9903.04.63` in [Revision 14](/blog/2026-hts-revision-14) is now 0%, and it reaches back to July 31, 2026, the start of the pharmaceutical tariffs.

## What changed

### UK patented pharmaceuticals (9903.04.63): 10% to 0%

Heading `9903.04.63` covers patented pharmaceutical articles that are products of the United Kingdom, as defined in U.S. note 40(c) and (g) to Chapter 99. The change record lists it as "Modified (rates of duty)", effective July 31, 2026.

| | Revision 14 | Revision 15 |
|---|---|---|
| Heading | `9903.04.63` | `9903.04.63` |
| General and Special rate | Applicable subheading duty + 10% | Applicable subheading duty + 0% |
| Column 2 | Applicable subheading duty | Unchanged |

The rules for when `9903.04.63` applies (note 40(g)) didn't change. UK patented pharmaceuticals still enter under their own heading, so they stay out of the up-to-100% rate under `9903.04.60` that applies to countries without a deal.

<Callout tone="primary" title="Effective from day one">
July 31, 2026 is the date the UK heading itself started. So the 0% rate covers the heading's whole life, and no entry under `9903.04.63` should have paid the 10%.
</Callout>

### Everything else

Nothing else in Chapter 99 changed in this revision. The other pharmaceutical headings, including those for the EU, Japan, Korea, Switzerland and Liechtenstein, kept their Revision 14 rates.

## What it means for importers

**On $10,000 of a UK patented medicine** entered on or after July 31, 2026:

| | Heading | Additional Section 232 duty |
|---|---|---|
| Under Revision 14 | `9903.04.63`: + 10% | $1,000 |
| Under Revision 15 | `9903.04.63`: + 0% | $0 |

If you entered UK patented pharmaceuticals between July 31 and August 2, while Revision 14 was current, and paid the 10%, look at those entries again. The change record dates the 0% rate to July 31, so they fall under it too, and any refund runs through CBP's normal processes, such as a post-summary correction or a protest.

For everyone else, Revision 15 doesn't change the math. The [next revision](/blog/2026-hts-revision-16), which added a Section 201 safeguard on quartz surface products, is a much bigger one. For later changes to the pharmaceutical tariff itself, including a new R&D exemption, see [Revision 20](/blog/2026-hts-revision-20), and for every 2026 revision, the [2026 HTS revisions hub](/blog/2026-hts-revisions).
