## What happened

An executive order on August 6, 2025 announced an additional tariff on goods from India, citing India's purchases of Russian oil. The order said the HTS would be updated to put it into effect, and it was: from **August 27, 2025**, most imports from India paid an extra **25%**.

This came on top of the 25% reciprocal tariff India already faced, which put a lot of Indian goods at 50% in additional duty before counting the base rate.

## The headings

The HTS change record for 2025 Revision 20 added six headings in Chapter 99, Subchapter III, under U.S. note 2(z): one for the tariff and five for its exemptions.

| Heading | What it covered |
|---|---|
| `9903.01.84` | The 25% additional duty on articles of India |
| `9903.01.85` | Goods already in transit before the effective date |
| `9903.01.86` | Critical goods listed in the order |
| `9903.01.87` | Articles subject to Section 232 duties |
| `9903.01.88` | Donations |
| `9903.01.89` | Informational materials |

The Section 232 exemption mattered most in practice. Steel, aluminum, copper and auto parts from India that already paid Section 232 didn't pay the extra 25% on top.

## How it ended

The tariff relied on the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA). On **February 20, 2026**, the Supreme Court held in *Learning Resources, Inc. v. Trump* that IEEPA doesn't authorize tariffs. That ruling covered every IEEPA tariff, this one included, and CBP made the IEEPA headings inactive from **February 24, 2026**.

Goods from India then paid the temporary Section 122 surcharge that replaced the IEEPA tariffs from February 24 until it expired on July 24, 2026, along with any Section 232 or other duties that applied to the product.

## If you imported from India during this period

Entries between August 27, 2025 and February 23, 2026 that paid `9903.01.84` paid an IEEPA duty. Refunds of IEEPA duties are being handled separately, so check with your customs broker on the status of your entries and keep your entry summaries and HTS classifications together.
