## Who it's for

- **Main customer:** mid to large-size US importers with 20 to 200+ products and the customs brokers, 3PLs, and freight forwarders who support them.

## The problems we solve

1. **"Overpaying on your imports"** Improper tariff stacking, unclaimed exemptions & trade agreements, rules change after entry,.
1. **"Missing or finding about tariff changes too late"** Tariffs change constantly, and finding out late means a
   surprise on the customs bill.
2. **"Not knowing what you'll pay for a new import"** The base rate, Chapter 99 tariffs, exemptions, trade programs, AD/CVD, and fees.
4. **"Misclassification"** A wrong HTS code can mean paying more duty increased audit risk.

## Rules we never break

**Legal**
- HTS Hero is not a customs broker. We find, flag and explain; you or your broker review and decide if you want to take action.
- We never file entries, corrections, protests or refund claims.
- "Audit" is fine to say. Never promise a filing, a classification, or that a shipment qualifies for something.
- Managed copy never promises "lowest-duty position" or "handling" changes.

**Savings and money**
- Savings are always potential: "could", "may", "up to", "may qualify". Never "you will save".
- Say "stop overpaying", never "never overpay".
- Rate changes are in %, before → after. Never dollar amounts in alerts or emails, and never "pts".
- Never invent a higher price to cross out.

**Features**
- ES-003 and 7501 upload is coming soon. Never say it's live.
- Never mention lookup limits or an "unlimited" paid calculator plan.
- Copy says "alert emails", never "users" or "seats".

**Emails**
- Value first, one clear next step, and always a path to sign up or upgrade.
- Buttons are full width. Never ask people to send their ACE report.
