## (a) How legal dates diverge from HTS revision dates
1. **Retroactive deal implementations.** The FR notice and HTS revision come weeks after the legal date:
   - Japan: Aug 7 rate, EO signed Sept 4.
   - EU: Aug 1 / Sept 1, notice ~Sept 25.
   - Korea: Nov 1 / Nov 14, notice Dec 4.
   - Switzerland: Nov 14, notice Dec 18.
   - Ag exemptions: Nov 13, EO dated Nov 14.
   - Apr 11 electronics exemption: retroactive to Apr 5.
2. **Future-dated actions.** The HTS publishes the heading before it is effective: Apr 2 proclamations effective Apr 6 (metals) and Jul 31 / Sept 29 (pharma); Proc. 10976 signed Sept 29, effective Oct 14.
3. **Same-day or overnight changes** with no HTS revision until days later: Apr 9/10 China escalations, Feb 24, 2026 IEEPA end.
4. **In-transit windows** (loaded-before / entered-before), which give a second date per heading: 9903.01.26; Aug 7 → Oct 5; India Aug 27 → Sept 17; Sec. 122 Feb 24 / Feb 28.
5. **Pauses and announced-but-not-effective rates:** Canada/Mexico Feb 4, furniture Jan 1, 2026.
6. **Heading text amended in place** with no new heading number (9903.01.24, 20%→10% on Nov 10). The engine needs versioned rate text, not just heading start/end dates.
7. **Retroactive expiry** of old headings (CH 9903.02.58 ends Nov 13).
