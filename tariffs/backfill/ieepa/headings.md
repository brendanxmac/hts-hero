# Chapter 99 headings 9903.01.xx / 9903.02.xx listed as "in effect" in 2026HTSRev20: IEEPA status
Prepared 2026-10-07. Scope: the 109 headings the coverage checker flags as in effect and not modeled. Research only; no repo files changed.
## Key official sources (read directly this session)
- **EO 14389, "Ending Certain Tariff Actions" (Feb 20, 2026)**, govinfo DCPD-202600131 (FR Feb 25, 2026, doc 2026-03832). Sec. 1: the IEEPA additional ad valorem duties under EO 14193, 14194, 14195, 14245, 14257, 14323, 14329, 14380 and 14382, each "as amended," "shall no longer be in effect and, as soon as practicable, shall no longer be collected." Sec. 2 lets Commerce, DHS and USTR modify the HTSUS by FR notice. The order does not set a date itself. It says de minimis (EO of Feb 20, 2026), the Sec. 122 surcharge, Sec. 232 and Sec. 301 are unaffected.
- **CBP CSMS #67834313 (sent Feb 22, 2026)**, https://content.govdelivery.com/bulletins/gd/USDHSCBP-40b11c9. IEEPA duties under EO 14193, 14194, 14195, 14245, 14257, 14323 and 14329, "including all modifications and amendments," end for goods entered or withdrawn "on or after 12:00 a.m. eastern time on February 24, 2026," and **"all HTSUS numbers applicable to the IEEPA tariffs will be inactive in ACE as of February 24, 2026."** Note the time: CSMS says **12:00 a.m.**, not 12:01 a.m.
- **FR doc 2025-23316 (90 FR 59281, Dec 18, 2025)**, Switzerland/Liechtenstein deal. The govinfo PDF shows it implements the deal under EO 14346 by modifying the reciprocal regime of EO 14257, as amended. That makes 9903.02.82–.91 IEEPA headings.
- **HTS 2026 Rev 20 text (local)**: the subchapter III notes and rows **do not** mark any of the 109 headings as terminated. No 9903.01/.02 row text changed after 2026 Rev 3; the only 2026 changes were the India 9903.01.84–.89 terminations as of Feb 7, 2026. Rev 20 does carry dated compiler's notes for other terminations: India (Feb 7, 2026), Sec. 122 9903.03.xx ("expired at the close of July 23, 2026," 91 FR 9339), Sec. 232 metals (Apr 6, 2026). USITC never published an equivalent note for the IEEPA headings, and that is why the checker reads them as "in effect." The legal end date has to come from EO 14389 and CSMS #67834313, not the HTS.

## How each heading maps to its authority
- Note numbers come from the Rev 20 heading text, and the Rev 20 note text was read for 2(a)–(c), (j)–(m), (s)–(v) and (x).
- The **parent EO** for each block follows from the note subdivision and the heading's country scope (Mexico 2(a)–(c); Canada 2(j)–(m); China 2(s)–(u); reciprocal 2(v); Brazil 2(x)). Every parent EO is named in EO 14389, so all 109 headings are covered.
- **Amending-EO numbers** in parentheses (e.g. EO 14325, 14228, 14357, 14231/14232) come from the existing `legal-timeline.md` notes or secondary sources. They do not change the conclusion, because EO 14389 and the CSMS cover each parent EO "as amended."
- Heading text cites an EO directly only for 9903.01.77/.78 (EO 14323) and 9903.02.01 (EO 14326, 90 FR 37963, which amends EO 14257).

## Summary
- **109 of 109 headings are IEEPA.** All were created by the IEEPA programs of EO 14193 (Canada), 14194 (Mexico), 14195 (China/HK), 14257 (reciprocal, including the EO 14326 country rates and transshipment rule and the EO 14346 Switzerland/Liechtenstein deal) and 14323 (Brazil). EO 14389 names every one of those EOs "as amended."
- By category: IEEPA duty: 78, IEEPA exemption: 27, IEEPA exemption/valuation rule: 1, IEEPA in-transit exemption: 3.
- **Duty headings:** 9903.01.01, .05, .10, .13, .15, .16, .24, .25, .77; 9903.02.01–.18, .21–.29, .31–.35, .37–.55, .57, .59–.71 (country rates); 9903.02.82/.83/.87/.88 (CH/LI deal rates).
- **Exemption, carve-out and in-transit headings** (donations, informational materials, USMCA, Annex II, Sec. 232 stacking, U.S. content, column-2 countries, civil aircraft, pharma, in-transit) only modify an IEEPA duty. They have no independent effect once that duty ends. **None of the 109 is a non-IEEPA provision that could still be in effect.**
- **End date:** all ended for goods entered or withdrawn on or after **12:00 a.m. ET, Feb 24, 2026** (CSMS #67834313; EO 14389). Entries dated up to Feb 23, 2026 were still assessed, then became refundable (CIT *Atmus*, CAPE refunds per `legal-timeline.md`). Two headings had already lapsed under their own text: 9903.01.23 (window closed 3/7/2025) and 9903.01.78 (window closed 10/5/2025).

## Items for the user's attention
1. **Effective time:** CSMS #67834313 says **12:00 a.m.** ET Feb 24, 2026. The task brief and some secondary sources say 12:01 a.m. EO 14389 gives no time. In both readings, entries dated Feb 24 or later carry no IEEPA duty, and entries dated Feb 23 or earlier do.
2. **The HTS was never annotated.** Through Rev 20 the HTS still prints these headings with no termination note, and the checker reads that as "in effect." Mark them expired in `libs/hts-coverage/initial-status.ts` with an explicit legal citation (EO 14389 / CSMS #67834313) rather than an HTS citation. Also resolve the existing `needsReview` entry "9903.01.21-24" the same way.
3. **Venezuelan-oil (EO 14245), Cuba (EO 14380) and Iran (EO 14382) programs:** none of the 109 headings implements them. They are not part of this list.
4. **Not in this list but related:** 9903.02.19/.20 (EU), .72–.81 (Japan/EU/Korea deal) are already handled in `deals.ts` with an end date of 2026-02-24 citing CSMS #67834313. Those headings rest on the same EO 14257/14346 basis.
5. **Amending-EO numbers marked "secondary" or "UNVERIFIED"** in the table are labels only. They do not affect the ended/IEEPA conclusion.
