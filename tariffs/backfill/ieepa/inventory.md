# IEEPA tariff inventory (Jan 2024 - 2026) -- research notes, 2026-10-07
Confidence: OFFICIAL = read on govinfo/whitehouse/CBP; SECONDARY = law firm/broker/news; UNVERIFIED = from memory or not checked this session.

## Key finding on the end date and time
- EO 14389 "Ending Certain Tariff Actions" (Feb 20, 2026), https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-202600131/html/DCPD-202600131.htm (OFFICIAL)
  - Terminates IEEPA duties under EOs 14193, 14194, 14195, 14245, 14257, 14323, 14329, 14380, 14382, including all amendments. The emergencies themselves stay in place.
  - The EO's own text gives NO clock time. It says the duties "shall no longer be in effect" and collection stops "as soon as practicable".
  - Exempts the de minimis EO (14388) and the Sec 122 proclamation.
- CBP CSMS #67834313, https://content.govdelivery.com/bulletins/gd/USDHSCBP-40b11c9 (OFFICIAL)
  - Sets the cutoff at **12:00 a.m. ET Feb 24, 2026** for goods entered or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption.
  - All IEEPA HTS numbers are deactivated, but the CSMS does not list them.
  - It names only 7 EOs and omits 14380/14382.
  - Several secondary sources say 12:01 a.m. That conflicts with CBP; 12:01 a.m. is the effective time of Sec 122 and EO 14388.

## 2024
- No IEEPA tariffs. The CBP and EO 14389 lists start at EO 14193 (Feb 1, 2025). Confidence: high, inferred from the absence of any 2024 order.

## Items checked
- EO 14245 Venezuela oil (Mar 24, 2025; usable from Apr 2, 2025)
  - A 25% framework that was NEVER applied to any country. Secondary sources: Cambridge AJIL note, Baker Botts tracker.
  - It still appears in the EO 14389 and CSMS termination lists.
- EO 14380 Cuba oil suppliers (Jan 29, 2026)
  - A framework only. No country determination was found before Feb 24 (SECONDARY / UNVERIFIED negative).
- EO 14382 Iran trading partners (Feb 6, 2026; effective 12:01 a.m. EST Feb 7)
  - A framework with "for example 25%". No country designation was found (SECONDARY / UNVERIFIED negative).
- EO 14384 (Feb 6, 2026)
  - Removed the India Russian-oil 25% (EO 14329) at 12:01 a.m. EST Feb 7, 2026.
  - Terminated 9903.01.84-.89 and U.S. Note 2(z). Source: whitehouse.gov/?p=35213 (OFFICIAL). CBP CSMS #67702087 (SECONDARY reference).
- EO 14357 (Nov 4, 2025): China fentanyl duty cut from 20% to 10% effective Nov 10, 2025, under 9903.01.24 (SECONDARY: KPMG, EY).
- EO 14360 (Nov 14, 2025): agricultural exemptions from the reciprocal tariff (SECONDARY; govinfo DCPD-202501123).
- Brazil EO of Nov 20, 2025: agricultural exemptions from the 40%, retroactive to 12:01 a.m. EST Nov 13, 2025 (SECONDARY; FR 2025-21417).
- EO 14345 Japan (Sept 4, 2025) and EO 14346 PTAAP (Sept 5, 2025) (SECONDARY).
- EO 14324 global de minimis suspension (Jul 30, 2025; 12:01 a.m. EDT Aug 29, 2025). Source: govinfo FR 2025-14897 (OFFICIAL).
  - Authority: IEEPA plus 19 USC 2483.
  - Postal duty choice of method:
    - ad valorem at the effective IEEPA rate, or
    - a specific duty of $80 (<16%), $160 (16-25%) or $200 (>25%) per item, available for 6 months only.
  - Supersedes EO 14256 sec. 2 for China/HK.
- EO 14388 continuing the de minimis suspension (Feb 20, 2026; 12:01 a.m. EST Feb 24, 2026). Source: govinfo DCPD-202600130 (OFFICIAL).
  - Authority: IEEPA plus 19 USC 2483. The suspension survives the SCOTUS ruling.
  - Postal duty is now ad valorem at the Sec 122 surcharge rate. That lasts until the earlier of the surcharge's end or CBP's new postal entry process.
- Section 122 proclamation (Feb 20, 2026): 10% from 12:01 a.m. EST Feb 24, 2026 for 150 days (through about Jul 24, 2026).
  - Headings 9903.03.01, with exemptions in 9903.03.02-.11. NOT IEEPA. (SECONDARY: GHY, AACB)
