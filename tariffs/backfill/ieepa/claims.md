# IEEPA tariff claims: verification notes (researched 2026-10-07)

All quotes below come from primary sources: govinfo DCPD texts of the EOs, CBP CSMS bulletins, the SCOTUS docket and slip opinion, CBP.gov, and the CIT order. Raw text copies are in this scratchpad under eo/, csms/, op.txt, atmus.pdf and cbp_refunds.txt.
URL patterns:
- EO text: https://www.govinfo.gov/link/cpd/executiveorder/<EO#>?link-type=html. This redirects to a DCPD page.
- CSMS: https://content.govdelivery.com/bulletins/gd/USDHSCBP-<hex(CSMS#)>. For example, 67834313 = 40b11c9.

## 1. Fentanyl/border tariffs (CONFIRMED: IEEPA)
Each of EO 14193, 14194 and 14195 (Feb 1, 2025) cites "the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (50 U.S.C. 1701 et seq.) (IEEPA)".

### Canada (EO 14193, 90 FR 9113)
- Original order: 25%, with energy at 10%. It was to take effect at 12:01 a.m. ET on Feb 4, 2025. Quote: "subject to an additional 10 percent ad valorem rate of duty" (energy).
- EO 14197 (Feb 3, 2025) paused it: "shall be paused and will not take effect until March 4, 2025, at 12:01 a.m. eastern time." The same order withdrew the in-transit exception.
- CSMS 64297449 (3/3/2025): "Effective on or after 12:01 a.m. eastern standard time on March 4, 2025 ... 9903.01.10". Energy is 9903.01.13. Exclusions are 9903.01.11 and .12.
- EO 14231 (Mar 6, 2025) added the USMCA exemption and set potash at 10%. Quote: "effective ... on or after 12:01 a.m. eastern standard time on March 7, 2025." CSMS 64336037 assigns USMCA goods to 9903.01.14 and potash to 9903.01.15.
- EO 14226 (Mar 2, 2025) dealt only with de minimis.
- EO 14325 (Jul 31, 2025) raised the rate from 25% to 35%. Quote: "on or after 12:01 a.m. eastern daylight time on August 1, 2025". Transshipped goods get 40% under 9903.01.16 (CSMS 65798609).
- CSMS 64514918 and 65054354 updated the energy scope. Not detailed here.

### Mexico (EO 14194, 90 FR 9117)
- Original order: 25%. EO 14198 paused it to Mar 4, 2025 at 12:01 a.m.
- CSMS 64297292: heading 9903.01.01, effective at 12:01 a.m. EST on Mar 4, 2025. Exclusions are .02 and .03.
- EO 14232 (Mar 6, 2025) added the USMCA exemption (9903.01.04) and set potash at 10% (9903.01.05), effective 12:01 a.m. EST Mar 7, 2025 (CSMS 64335789).
- I found no CSMS that implemented any later change to the Mexico rate. The 30% increase that was announced is UNVERIFIED as implemented; it appears never to have taken effect.

### China and Hong Kong (EO 14195, 90 FR 9121)
- Original order: 10%, effective 12:01 a.m. ET Feb 4, 2025. CSMS 63988468: "9903.01.20: All imports of articles that are products of China and Hong Kong ... 10%". Exclusions are .21 to .23.
- EO 14228 (Mar 3, 2025) changed "10 percent" to "20 percent". The EO text gives no time. CSMS 64299816 does: "on or after 12:01 a.m. eastern standard time on March 4, 2025: 9903.01.24".
- EO 14200 (Feb 5, 2025) and EO 14256 (Apr 2, 2025) dealt with de minimis and postal duties. EO 14256 ended de minimis for PRC and HK from 12:01 a.m. EDT May 2, 2025, and set postal duties of 30%, or $25 rising to $50 per item.
- EO 14357 (Nov 4, 2025) cut the rate from 20% to 10%. Quote: "on or after 12:01 a.m. eastern standard time on November 10, 2025: heading 9903.01.24 is amended by deleting '20%' ... inserting '10%'". CSMS 66749380 matches.

## 2. Brazil (CONFIRMED: IEEPA, EO 14323, Jul 30, 2025, 90 FR 37739)
- Rate: 40%. Quote: "effective ... on or after 12:01 a.m. eastern daylight time 7 days after the date of this order". That date is Aug 6, 2025.
- In-transit exception: goods loaded before that time and entered "before 12:01 a.m. eastern daylight time on October 5, 2025."
- CSMS 65807735: the duty heading is 9903.01.77, effective 12:01 a.m. EDT Aug 6, 2025. In-transit goods use 9903.01.78. Annex I exemptions and civil aircraft use 9903.01.79 to .83. The duty does not apply to goods subject to Section 232.
- EO 14361 (Nov 20, 2025) exempted agricultural products retroactively to 12:01 a.m. EST Nov 13, 2025. Filers use 9903.01.81, or 9903.01.90 for the note 2(x)(iii)(b) categories (CSMS 66871909).

## 3. India (CONFIRMED: IEEPA, EO 14329, Aug 6, 2025, 90 FR 38701)
- Rate: 25%, effective 12:01 a.m. EDT "21 days after the date of this order". That date is Aug 27, 2025.
- In-transit exception: goods entered "before 12:01 a.m. eastern daylight time on September 17, 2025."
- CSMS 66027027: duty heading 9903.01.84. The in-transit and exemption headings run from 9903.01.85 to 9903.01.89.
- REMOVED BEFORE SCOTUS by EO 14384 (Feb 6, 2026). Quote: "on or after 12:01 a.m. eastern standard time on February 7, 2026, products of India ... shall no longer be subject to the additional ad valorem rate of duty of 25 percent". CSMS 67702087 adds: "headings 9903.01.84-9903.01.89 are no longer in use as of February 7, 2026."
- EO 14384 says any refunds go through "standard procedures". It is not retroactive.
- Note: EO 14329 is a Russia-oil order, and India was the only country listed under it.

## 4. End of IEEPA tariffs
### (a) Decision
- SCOTUS docket 24-1287: "Feb 20 2026 Judgment in No. 24-1287 is VACATED and case REMANDED with instructions to dismiss for lack of jurisdiction; the judgment in No. 25-250 is AFFIRMED."
- Syllabus: "Held: IEEPA does not authorize the President to impose tariffs."
- 24-1287 was dismissed for lack of jurisdiction because the District Court lacked jurisdiction and the CIT has exclusive jurisdiction. The merits ruling came through 25-250 (V.O.S. Selections), which affirmed the Federal Circuit decision at 149 F.4th 1312.
- Docket: "Mar 24 2026 Judgment Issued." (this is for 24-1287)
- CSMS 68340863: "On March 2, 2026, the Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit (CAFC) issued its formal mandate to the Court of International Trade".
- So the ruling was announced Feb 20, but it took legal effect through formal judgments and mandates issued in March. The government did not wait for those. It stopped collecting voluntarily under EO 14389.

### (b) When collection stopped
- EO 14389 (Feb 20, 2026, DCPD-202600131, published in the FR on Feb 25) says the duties under EOs 14193, 14194, 14195, 14245, 14257, 14323, 14329, 14380 and 14382 "shall no longer be in effect and, as soon as practicable, shall no longer be collected." The EO gives no date or time.
- CSMS 67834313 (sent 02/22/2026 at 9:57 PM EST): "will no longer be in effect and will no longer be collected for goods entered for consumption or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after 12:00 a.m. eastern time on February 24, 2026". It adds that "all HTSUS numbers applicable to the IEEPA tariffs will be inactive in ACE as of February 24, 2026."
- The cutoff is **12:00 a.m.** ET, not 12:01. The Section 122 surcharge starts at 12:01 a.m. EST Feb 24 (SECONDARY source).

### (c) Entries dated Feb 20 to 23, 2026
- IEEPA duties were still collected on these entries, because ACE kept the headings active until Feb 24 (inference from the CSMS).
- They are refundable like any other IEEPA entry. Atmus order (CIT 26-01259, Mar 4, 2026): "with respect to any and all unliquidated entries that were entered subject to the IEEPA duties, U.S. Customs and Border Protection is hereby directed to liquidate those entries without regard to the IEEPA duties. Any liquidated entries for which liquidation is not final shall be reliquidated without regard to IEEPA duties."
- No source singles out Feb 20 to 23. These entries are simply part of "all" IEEPA entries.

### (d) Refunds
- Mar 4, 2026: the CIT Atmus order quoted above.
- Mar 6, 2026: the court suspended the order's immediacy requirement (CSMS 68340863).
- Mar 27, 2026: the order was extended to finally liquidated entries (SECONDARY; Thompson Hine).
- Apr 7, 2026: Euro-Notions order (CSMS 68340863).
- Apr 20, 2026: CAPE Phase 1 deployed in ACE at 8 AM EDT. It covers unliquidated entries and entries within 80 days after liquidation.
- Later phases: reconciliation-flagged entries (June 29, 2026 deployment), with more phases under evaluation as of 9/2/2026.
- Interest is paid under 19 U.S.C. 1505. Refunds generally arrive 60 to 90 days after CBP accepts the CAPE Declaration (https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies/ieepa-duty-refunds).

## 5. Earlier ends and suspensions
- India 25%: ended 12:01 a.m. EST Feb 7, 2026 (EO 14384, CSMS 67702087).
- China fentanyl rate: cut from 20% to 10% on Nov 10, 2025. This was a reduction, not an end.
- Reciprocal tariffs (EO 14257):
  - Country-specific rates were suspended from 12:01 a.m. EDT Apr 10, 2025 to Jul 9, 2025, with a 10% baseline in place (EO 14266).
  - The suspension was later extended to Aug 7, 2025 (CSMS 65829726). Not verified in detail.
  - Some agricultural products were exempted from 12:01 a.m. EST Nov 13, 2025 (EO 14360).
  - Deal-based changes: Japan (CSMS 66146676 and 66242844, retroactive), EU (66336270), Korea (66987366), Switzerland and Liechtenstein (67133044).
  - Under the US–China deal, China's suspended reciprocal portion (9903.01.63) stays suspended until Nov 10, 2026.
  - None of these ended the IEEPA tariffs entirely before Feb 24, 2026, apart from India.
- The CIT judgment of May 28, 2025 in V.O.S. did not stop collection. The Federal Circuit stayed it (CSMS 65201384; SECONDARY for the stay).

## Recommendation
- "Assessed on entry date X": end every IEEPA heading's effective period at entry or warehouse-withdrawal date **< 2026-02-24 00:00 ET**, exclusive. India 9903.01.84 to .89 instead ends at < 2026-02-07 00:01 ET.
- "Legally owed after refunds": $0 for all IEEPA headings for every entry date, i.e. void from the start. Do not model this as an end date of Feb 20. Model it as a separate legal-status flag ("invalidated, Learning Resources, decided 2026-02-20; refundable per Atmus CIT order 2026-03-04").
- Do not use Feb 20 as the end date for assessment. Duties were actually assessed and collected through Feb 23, and they are refundable just like the duties paid before Feb 20.
