## Change Record

The modifications set forth in this change record reflect updates made to the HTS after 2026 Revision 5, published April 8, 2026. Note that the Change Record does not include staged rates, endnotes or minor nonsubstantive adjustments to the format of any line. The modifications set forth in this change record reflect updates made to the HTS since 2026 Revision 5 was posted:

Chapter 99.

**Notes:** For purposes of this record, the terms “added” and “deleted” refer to the insertion or removal of 8-digit (legal) tariff rate lines or legal notes; the terms “established” or “discontinued” refer to the insertion or removal of 10-digit (nonlegal) statistical reporting numbers or notes. The term “annotated” refers to the creation of new 10-digit provisions under a previously unsubdivided rate line; the term “restored” refers to the deletion of all annotations under an existing rate line. The term “modified” may be used with changes in any provision or note.
