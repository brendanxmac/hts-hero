| <b>Term</b> | <b>Meaning</b>                                                                                                                                                             |
|-------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Annotated   | Establishment of two or more 10-digit statistical reporting numbers under the listed HTS number, where it was previously not subdivided into statistical reporting numbers |
| Deleted     | Removal of an HTS number or note                                                                                                                                           |
| Established | Insertion of a new HTS number or note                                                                                                                                      |
| Expired     | Cessation of legal effect of provision based upon a previously scheduled end date                                                                                          |
| Modified    | Change(s) to existing legal or statistical text in the HTS                                                                                                                 |
| Restored    | Reactivation of a provision that was previously annotated, terminated, suspended or expired                                                                                |
| Renumbered  | Redesignation of existing legal or statistical text to a new alphabetical or numerical subdivision                                                                         |
| Suspended   | The legal text is no longer in effect for a temporary period of time; the suspension period may, or may not, be known                                                      |
| Errata      | Rectification of a clerical error or minor editorial alteration so that the text corresponds with the proclaimed source document which modified the HTS                    |
| Terminated  | The legal text is no longer in effect                                                                                                                                      |
