For the purposes of this note:
    (i) “Pharmaceutical articles” refers to imported articles classifiable in the provisions enumerated in this subdivision that are pharmaceutical products or that are ingredients (active pharmaceutical ingredients and key starting materials) classifiable in the provisions enumerated in this subdivision used to make pharmaceutical products.
